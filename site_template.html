Treaty-Territory Data Center Watch · Minnesota pilot

Data center proposals published in Minnesota's weekly EQB Monitor, checked against reservation, trust land and treaty cession boundaries, with review stage and comment deadlines. Built so tribal staff and community members don't have to wait for a developer or a local government to tell them.

Early warning, not an authority. Treaty cession areas come from 1890s maps (Royce) digitized by the U.S. Forest Service and are approximate. Project locations are city or township centers unless noted. Tribal nations, not this map, define their territories and rights. Public data only. Always confirm with the official notice.
