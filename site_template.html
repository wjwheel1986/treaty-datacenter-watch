Treaty-Territory Data Center Watch

Data center proposals announced in Minnesota's official weekly environmental notices (the EQB Monitor), shown next to reservations and land ceded by treaty, so tribal staff and community members can see what's coming and when they can weigh in.

  1. 1Choose a tribal nation, or leave it on "All of Minnesota."
  2. 2Each dot on the map is a project. Blue = confirmed data center, orange = possible, green diamond = power line for one.
  3. 3Click a project to see what it is, where it is, whether you can still comment, and the official notices.
Read first: what this map can and can't tell you

An early warning, not an authority. Treaty cession areas come from 1890s maps (Royce) digitized by the U.S. Forest Service and are approximate. Tribal nations, not this map, define their territories and rights, and the rights kept differ by treaty. Project dots sit at city, township or county centers, not exact sites. "Possible" projects are flagged from wording signals and need a person to confirm. Public data only: the map never shows cultural, sacred or harvest sites. Always check the official notice. How it was built and tested.

Shows projects within 50 miles of the reservation, or inside a treaty cession area that also covers part of it.
